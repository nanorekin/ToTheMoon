How to play

Goal

Swipe, use the arrow keys, or tap the on-screen pad to slide every coin on the board in one direction. Two coins of the same kind merge into the next coin up the ladder. Reach an XNO tile to win the round; keep playing afterwards for a higher score.

The ladder

Dogecoin → Litecoin → XRP → Cardano → BNB → Solana → Polkadot → Monero → Ethereum → Bitcoin → Nano. Merging two Nano tiles makes a gold "XNO ×2" tile, and so on.

Scoring

Each merge scores the value of the coin it creates, so later merges are worth far more than early ones.

Modes

Daily: the same starting board and coin draws for everyone, new at 00:00 UTC. Play until the board is full, or finish early once you've reached Nano. Blitz 90: 90 seconds on the clock — if the board fills up, it clears itself and your score carries on. Endless: free play with no clock; the round ends when the board is full and no merges are left.

Undo

Made a bad move? Undo it once per round, free of charge. Supporters get two undos.

Ranks

Reaching an XNO tile earns a win. Total wins give a rank: Newbie, Hodler (5), Diamond Hands (15), Whale (40) and Satoshi (100).

Supporters

A tip of 0.01 XNO or more unlocks the extra accent colours and a second undo. Tips are optional and the whole game is free.
