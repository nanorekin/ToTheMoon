How to play

Goal

Your rocket fires automatically. Drag your finger (or mouse) left and right across the board to steer, or use the arrow keys / A and D. Blast asteroids before they reach the bottom of the screen — each one that gets past you, or hits your rocket, costs a life.

Splitting asteroids

Large asteroids break into two medium ones when hit, and medium asteroids break into two small ones. Only small asteroids are destroyed outright. Smaller pieces move faster but are worth more, so clearing a whole cluster pays off.

Power-up coins

A glowing coin drifts down now and then — shoot it or fly into it, no danger if you miss it. A BTC coin gives your rocket a steady beam for a few seconds: stand under asteroids and it destroys them continuously, no shooting needed. An ETH coin gives rapid fire — faster, wider shots for a few seconds. A pink coin is an extra life (or a score bonus if you're already full). An XNO coin is the strongest: it instantly clears every asteroid on screen.

Scoring

Points scale up with your combo — asteroids destroyed back to back without taking a hit. Getting hit, or letting one slip past, resets the combo.

Modes

Daily: the same asteroid field for everyone, new at 00:00 UTC. Destroy 40 to clear the day. Quick 60: 60 seconds, score as much as you can. Survival: endless, asteroids get faster and more frequent — how long can you last?

Lives and ranks

You start with 3 lives (4 for supporters), with a short shield after each hit. Total asteroids destroyed earns a rank: Newbie, Hodler (200), Diamond Hands (700), Whale (2,000) and Satoshi (5,000).

Supporters

A tip of 0.01 XNO or more unlocks the extra accent colours and a 4th life. Tips are optional and the whole game is free.
